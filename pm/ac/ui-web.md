### Block `ui-web`: a GUI with a web or PWA target

Every screen the ticket adds or changes on a web or PWA target, in addition to `ui`.

- At every width the product supports, from the narrowest up, nothing overlaps or is cut off and the page has no horizontal scroll.
- Every interactive control is reachable by keyboard in a sensible order and shows visible focus.
- Reloading the page keeps the viewer where they were: the same screen, record, section, filters and sort.
- The browser's back button returns to the previous screen with its state, never leaving the product or losing entered input without asking.
- Every screen the ticket names has its own address: opening that address directly (a deep link) shows the screen, and one the viewer may not see shows sign-in or not-found.
