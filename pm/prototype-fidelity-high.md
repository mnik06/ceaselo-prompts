## Fidelity: high

This version is polished and close to the shipped product: a client should be able to put it in front of their own users.

- The kit renders it at the brief's accent, mood, density and radius; every decision on the screen is deliberate and consistent across screens.
- Considered hierarchy: each screen has one job, one primary action and a clear reading order; secondary actions sit in menus or ghost buttons; dense data is grouped and scannable.
- Rich realistic data from the shared data module: full tables (15–25 rows) with varied lengths, edge values, every status, relative and absolute dates, totals that add up across screens.
- Every state and micro-interaction is shown as its own state block: inline validation with specific error text, a disabled submit until the form is valid, a busy button while saving, a success toast after it, optimistic updates in lists, hover and selected rows, confirmation dialogs for destructive actions, permission-limited views per role.
- Copy is final-quality: precise labels, helpful hints, errors that say how to fix the problem, empty states that teach the next step, all in the brief's voice.
- Mobile screens are designed for the phone first: bottom tab bar or clear back navigation, lists instead of wide tables, thumb-reachable primary actions.
