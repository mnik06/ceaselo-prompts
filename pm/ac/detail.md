### Block `detail`: record detail screen

- The record's identity (name, key or title) is visible without scrolling.
- Every field the record has is shown, with an explicit placeholder for an empty field.
- A record that does not exist, was deleted, or the viewer may not see shows a not-found screen with a way back, never a broken screen.
- Opening the record directly (from a link, a notification, or on returning to the product) shows it, including the section that was open.
- Where the ticket offers editing, edits made on the screen persist: they are still there when the record is opened again.
- Where the ticket offers deleting, it asks for confirmation naming the record and what else is removed with it, then lands on the list with a confirmation.
