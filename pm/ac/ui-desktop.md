### Block `ui-desktop`: a GUI on desktop OSes (macOS, Windows, Linux)

Every window, panel or dialog the ticket adds or changes on a desktop target, in addition to `ui`.

- Resizing the window down to its minimum size (the ticket states it) leaves nothing overlapping or cut off; the window cannot be made smaller than that.
- Every keyboard shortcut the ticket names performs its action and is shown beside the action in its menu or tooltip.
- Every interactive control is reachable by keyboard in a sensible order and shows visible focus; Escape closes a dialog without saving.
