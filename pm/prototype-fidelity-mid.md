## Fidelity: mid

This version is clean, consistent product styling: the client should recognise their product and judge both the flow and the look.

- The kit renders it at the brief's accent, mood, density and radius; express the brand through those tokens and the copy, never through extra styling.
- Real copy everywhere: labels, help text, errors and empty states in the product's own words and the brief's voice.
- Realistic sample data from the shared data module: believable names, amounts, dates and statuses, enough rows to show how a real list reads (10–20), including long values and edge cases.
- Clear visual hierarchy on every screen: page header, one primary action, supporting content grouped in sections or cards where grouping means something.
- Simple visuals: stat tiles, `.bars` and `.meter` from the sample data, badges for status, initials avatars. No imagery beyond `.placeholder` blocks.
- Every planned state is a complete, styled screen with its message and next step.
